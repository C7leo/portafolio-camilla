# Editorial direction from the supplied references

Four distinct designs were visually supplied; the Hudak reference was repeated. The references guide graphic relationships rather than borrowing their brand names, unrelated projects, portraits, fictional metrics or testimonials.

## 1. Libertans

The hero mixes a generous white field with a compressed, multiline central headline. Product cutouts interrupt its edges. A highlighted word changes the reading rhythm, while small labels sit far from the dominant type. The next dark field compresses the composition into a denser visual register.

Translation: an asymmetric introduction with a narrow marginal column, oversized CODE and DATA, a small grotesk connector and an interface crop crossing the headline. Projects transition to a dense dark plate. Lime belongs to specific graphic emphasis, not every button.

## 2. Carbon

The top composition uses three unequal tracks, a large acid-yellow circle, an image broken into rounded and curved windows, and smaller fragments that preserve a shared visual alignment. Typography, photos and statistical scales occupy different amounts of space. Lower sections change rhythm instead of repeating the same grid.

Translation: About uses a large circle intersecting a twelve-window mask of the actual tracing study. The text is a separate editorial column. The academic cycle is a small factual annotation in the reading flow, not a dashboard metric. No invented portrait or NFT imagery is imported.

## 3. Hudak

Tall condensed lettering operates like poster type. Portraits interrupt word shapes and shift away from the grid. A perspective construction grid creates a stage for a scattered collection. Dark and light spreads alternate, with isolated violet and acid-green emphasis and an oversized cropped footer wordmark.

Translation: the project uses a perspective grid, tilted actual prototype capture and overlapping A LITTLE PROGRESS lettering. The working tracing interface follows as a separate hands-on spread. The giant footer name is deliberately cropped; project status, contribution and controls remain fully readable outside the graphic overlap.

## 4. Qorry

A narrow left margin of tags and a large arrow balances a dominant blue poster. Letterforms rotate and crop into the image boundary. Thin horizontal rules, micro labels and unequal résumé columns organize the information below without relying on a uniform card system.

Translation: the hero and closing spread have narrow marginal columns. Contact is a blue typographic poster with rotated, staggered words, while actual links and pending contact information sit outside its mask. Experience uses three unequal columns with fine rules, verified sequence labels and no invented dates.

## Material and motion

No authentic portrait, application repository screenshot or original cat asset is available. Images are direct captures of the already implemented portfolio interaction, explicitly captioned as such. Photography from the references is not repurposed as Camilla or her work. The mascot remains a small pending signature.

The existing reversible section covers and directional wipes are preserved. Source imagery receives a restrained mask reveal and scroll parallax. Reduced motion removes pinning and masks, leaving the full static editorial composition accessible. All professional content, project status, modal, drawing controls and technology selection remain intact.
