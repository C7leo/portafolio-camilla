# Servicios

`profile/presentation/services/ServicesComponent` presents the profile's freelance offering between Projects and Experience. It shares the existing `.container`, Urbanist scale, brand tokens, `EditorialGridComponent` and `RevealDirective`. The white neutral surface supports three equal-height cards: a violet featured Web card and two #F2F2F2 cards for Software and Data, all with a 16 px radius and no shadows or borders. Small 24 px Lucide icons sit at the upper left; labels, headings and descriptions occupy the lower area. Tablet uses two columns with the last card spanning the row; mobile stacks all three. In dark mode the neutral surface and gray cards follow the shared dark tokens while violet and lime retain their brand colors.

The section owns contextual neutral rails; the viewport grid excludes that surface to avoid double lines. Horizontal rules follow the section label and frame the compact CTA beneath the cards. The last 1 px rule sits inside the section edge so the following Experience surface cannot cover it. The CTA uses 32 px vertical padding (28 px on mobile), a small label and a secondary title aligned with the lime action; it has no fixed/minimum height or trailing section padding. Reveals use the shared motion observer with 90 ms stagger steps. Hover raises a card by 3 px and nudges the CTA arrow; reduced motion disables both effects. Protected text participates in the existing utility collision system.

Hero and Services consume the same `freelanceWhatsappUrl` from the profile application layer, preserving the configured number and encoded message. The external CTA has a 44 px minimum height, keyboard focus and `noopener noreferrer`. Labels and content use the ES/EN dictionary. Navbar and footer expose `#servicios`; Experience is now section 05. Services enters and leaves in natural flow, preserving the previous Projects-to-Experience behavior and Experience's own cinematic journey.

`tests/services.spec.js` verifies 320, 390, 768, 821, 1024 and 1440 px layouts, equal desktop heights, card colors, SVG icons, guide alignment, document order, ES/EN navigation, contact configuration, keyboard focus, overflow and forward/reverse scroll continuity with and without reduced motion. Screenshots are saved with the Playwright results.
