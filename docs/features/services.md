# Servicios

`profile/presentation/services/ServicesComponent` presents the profile's freelance offering between Projects and Experience. It shares the existing `.container`, Urbanist scale, brand tokens, `EditorialGridComponent` and `RevealDirective`. The white neutral surface supports three equal-height cards: a violet featured Web card and two #F2F2F2 cards for Software and Data, all with a 16 px radius and no shadows or borders. Small 24 px Lucide icons sit at the upper left; labels, headings and descriptions occupy the lower area. Tablet uses two columns with the last card spanning the row; mobile stacks all three. In dark mode the neutral surface and gray cards follow the shared dark tokens while violet and lime retain their brand colors.

The section owns contextual neutral rails; the viewport grid excludes that surface to avoid double lines. Horizontal rules follow the section label and frame the compact CTA beneath the cards. The last 1 px rule sits inside the section edge so the following Experience surface cannot cover it. The CTA uses 32 px vertical padding (28 px on mobile), a small label and a secondary title aligned with the lime action; it has no fixed/minimum height or trailing section padding. Below 641 px, the same contact link stacks 20 px below the title, aligned left with intrinsic width and a 44 px touch target. Reveals use the shared motion observer with 90 ms stagger steps. Hover raises a card by 3 px and nudges the CTA arrow; reduced motion disables both effects. Global floating controls remain available over the section.

Hero and Services link to the integrated `#contacto` form using `ContactLinkDirective`. It preserves standard anchor behavior for modified clicks and adds smooth viewport navigation with focus transfer for ordinary activation; reduced motion navigates immediately. The CTA retains its 44 px minimum height and keyboard focus. Labels and content use the ES/EN dictionary. Navbar and footer expose `#servicios`; Experience is section 05. Services enters and leaves in natural flow, preserving the Projects-to-Experience behavior and Experience's own cinematic journey.

The former dependency on a configured WhatsApp destination has been removed. The same CTA now renders unconditionally on mobile and desktop, without a personal telephone number or public Gmail address. See [Formspree contact](../formspree-contact.md).

`tests/services.spec.js` verifies 320, 390, 768, 821, 1024 and 1440 px layouts, equal desktop heights, card colors, SVG icons, guide alignment, document order, ES/EN navigation, contact configuration, keyboard focus, overflow and forward/reverse scroll continuity with and without reduced motion. Screenshots are saved with the Playwright results.
