# Floating controls and themes

`shell/controls/FloatingControlsComponent` lives outside the pinned chapters. It replaces the former Hero-only cue with one fixed scroll capsule and a separate theme button. Lucide supplies ArrowDown, Sun and Moon; labels use the existing ES/EN dictionary. The theme icons use an 18 px thin stroke inside a 44 px circle, with muted neutral ink, a faint border and soft shadow. The circle follows the white/deep-black surface token. Sun continues to indicate light mode and Moon dark mode; the accessible label describes the action.

The controls follow the existing editorial inset, retain 44 px hit targets, account for `safe-area-inset-bottom`, and adapt to small viewports. Within 40 px of the document end, the scroll cue rotates upward and becomes “Volver al inicio” / “Back to top”. Clicking it animates scroll to zero over 1.35 seconds beneath a temporary viewport blur veil, then clears the blur and focuses the main Home landmark without scrolling again. The veil does not filter or rebuild pinned chapters or canvas nodes. Repeated activation is disabled during the transition, and the GSAP timeline is killed on destruction. Reduced motion returns immediately without blur. The cue is hidden during the intro and modal/menu interactions and leaves the tab order while hidden. Elsewhere, clicking continues the current scroll.

Placement reads are batched in one animation frame. On Home, the lime scroll capsule follows a real responsive layout slot: below the lower band on desktop and between its links on mobile. It switches to the bottom left from About onward and remains available in Projects, Experience and the footer while more content exists and its area is clear. It cannot remain over an incoming section or overlap protected content. Short desktop viewports compact spacing without changing name sizes.

The theme button retains one fixed bottom-right position and the existing safe-area inset. Decorative lines and section boundaries do not affect its visibility. It hides only for modal/menu interactions or genuinely obstructing protected content; hidden controls leave the tab order. It returns in the same position on a clear surface. The document's remaining height and modal/menu states are still respected. Experience's reading-mode button retains its reserved space.

The shell declares `data-control-region` on its existing chapter hosts. `data-control-avoid` declares protected content. Containers containing links/buttons are resolved to those controls and their text, so empty padding and flex gaps do not become obstacles. The target cache excludes outgoing pinned regions covered by the incoming scene, and intersects obstacle bounds with their clipping ancestors so invisible card extensions cannot hide a control in the gutter. Two IntersectionObservers restricted to the scroll/theme button areas detect transformed cards entering them without a render-frame polling loop. A single delayed check follows scrubbed scenes; observers, timers and listeners are disposed on destruction. `data-control-surface` retains contextual contrast detection. See [the containment audit](../layout-containment-audit.md) for measured causes and affected files.

`shared/theme/ThemeService` owns the light/dark state and the `portfolio-theme` localStorage preference. First visits default to light regardless of OS theme. A small synchronous script in `index.html` restores the saved attribute before Angular and the first paint; critical HTML background styles prevent a white flash. The service supports blocked storage and changes from another tab. `data-theme` and the browser theme-color meta tag stay in sync.

Semantic CSS tokens live in `src/styles.css`: `--surface`, `--surface-raised`, `--text-primary`, `--text-editorial`, `--text-muted`, `--border-neutral`, neutral action colors and glass colors. Dark neutral surfaces use #09090B. White, ink, violet, pink and lime identity tokens keep their original meaning. Neutral color transitions take 350 ms, or 0 ms with reduced motion.

The About surfaces, neutral project dialog, Experience opening and navbar consume these tokens. Project violet, the Hero lime CTA, the About lime block, photos, film, footer and night valley keep their colors. No page-wide inversion is used. Existing monochrome technology icons alone adjust their monochrome treatment for contrast.

Theme changes do not dispatch resize events, refresh ScrollTrigger, rebuild GSAP timelines, replace canvas nodes or rewind the intro. About’s neutral planet changes through its existing renderer’s surface-color uniform; atmosphere colors, time, scroll and pointer state are retained. Its fallback uses the same neutral surface token.

Validation: `tests/floating-controls.spec.js` is configured for Chrome and WebKit at 375, 390, 393, 430, 768 and 1440 px. It checks colors, icon states, keyboard activation, unchanged layout/canvas identity, persistence, pre-bootstrap restoration, blocked storage, reduced motion, document-end visibility and Experience hit areas. WebKit automation does not replace checking browser chrome and safe areas on a physical iPhone.

The containment audit was executed in Chrome at responsive desktop/tablet/mobile sizes. `tests/layout-containment.spec.js` covers marquee aperture/loop geometry, toolbar layout, persistent visibility across decorative rules, real content collisions and stable theme restoration. Physical iOS/Safari validation remains separate.

Regression: `Projects utilities remain visible across every editorial line` positions the navigation rules directly through both fixed hit areas, checks ES/EN, keyboard theme switching, scroll activation and modal dismissal. See [the root cause report](../projects-controls-root-cause.md).
